# record-rand

Research & Development on the Record Layer of the Janus Facing Architecture.

The record of every exchange, held by six parties and hashed to a public chain. Nothing is erased; mistakes are corrected by annotation.

Research is discussed in #rand-record and voted on in #federation-record in the NTARI Slack workspace.

## Structure

| Tier | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Tier 1: The Tell Protocol](tier-1-tell-protocol/) | Understand how exchanges become records |
| [Tier 2: The Beacon Orchestrator](tier-2-beacon-orchestrator/) | Understand how records federate across geography without moving value |
| [Tier 3: The Route Platform](tier-3-route-platform/) | Implement the record as a market for storage that pays the prosumers who keep it |

## How we research

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your file to the correct tier folder.
3. Create a pull request with a [DCO signature](https://github.com/apps/dco).
4. Wait for peer review.

#federation-record deliberates your publication through testing, with no deadline: a pull request can stay open as long as it needs to, and a solution may wait dormant for years before it answers an emerging problem. While it is open you defend your claims and make the changes reviewers ask for. Follow the [Research & Development Submission Guide](https://ntari.slack.com/docs/T0978JCFFSL/F0BTB5N93A9): the method, the ethics check and the submission template.

Merged research is published in #federation-volunteers, on [www.ntari.org](https://www.ntari.org) and affiliated social media channels, with the author(s) credited.
